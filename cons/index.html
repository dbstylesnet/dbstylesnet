ABOUT US

Consensus was established in 2007 in response to the growing demand from foreign companies for business advisory services in Poland.
Our experience on a wide range of subjects and our extensive network of international contacts has allowed us to develop business relationships across the European Union and North Africa. Our goal is to bring companies together.

In addition, Consensus specialises in advising on issues of labour law in the European Union countries. We provide comprehensive support services to companies operating outside the country in question, as well as assisting in the recruitment of foreign workers.
Services
Services

Research and analysis
We offer comprehensive market research based on a wide variety of data collection methods. We analyse the market and the competition.

Advisory services
We provide consulting services regarding the search and selection of business partners in the European Union. This service helps in finding business partners, providing legal assistance and translation. We also represent our clients in meetings and negotiations.

Legal services
We offer services in the field of labour law related to work carried out within the European Union countries. We offer a wide range of services related to customer service, including the preparation of required applications and documents, and representation of our clients before administrative bodies.
